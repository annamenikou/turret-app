Is the airway noisy or silent?

Scene & Safety Management: Before you start

Upon arrival

  • Note the time of arrival
  • Tell control
  • Call for help or backup early

Safety 1-2-3

  • You
  • Environment
  • Patient

Read the scene

  • Consider the MOI
  • ≈90% of the diagnosis
C A B C D E

Noisy = partially obstructed

  • Bubbling → check for fluid
  • Snoring → tongue blockage
  • Check hard structures (jaw, cheekbones, teeth)
  • Check soft tissue (lips, tongue, larynx, nose — milk for signs of bleeding)
  • Open the mouth and clear foreign objects
  • For blood / vomit: postural drainage, then suction

Silent = complete obstruction

  • Back blows
  • Chest thrusts

Create an airway

  • Jaw thrust
  • Chin lift

If struggling, consider

  • ×2 NPA (6 Female / 7 Male)
  • OP tube
  • Keep going manually
