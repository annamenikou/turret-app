If on the floor

  • Stop all external bleeding
  • Direct pressure
  • Haemostatic gauze to junctional bleeds
  • Elevation & indirect pressure
  • Windlass dressing; splint long bones and pelvis

If on chest or abdomen

  • Surgeon needed ASAP

If on pelvis

  • Surgeon needed ASAP
  • Splint if unstable
  • Do NOT rock

If on long bones

  • Femur alignment (hip, knee, 2nd toe)

Signs of internal bleeding

  • Look for pattern bruising

Check radial pulse

  • Check both sides
  • Check the rate
  • Check the volume
  • Check skin colour (pale & clammy)
  • Capillary refill time (CRT) < 2s

No pulse?

  • Legs to 45° (only if no femur / pelvis fracture)
  • Preserve the first clot — apply careful pressure
