C A B C D E
C Catastrophic Bleeding Stop massive bleeding, stabilise the head & neck, check consciousness. › A Airway Is the airway noisy or silent? Clear it and create one. › B Breathing Expose the chest, assess rate & volume, treat wounds. › C Circulation Locate the blood, control it, check the pulse. › D Disability Reassess every 5 min: movement, AVPU, pupils, pain. › E Exposure / Evacuation Keep warm, pack, wrap, evacuate. Avoid hypothermia. ›
